Robotxian (roh-BOT-zee-an)

[Plural]: Robotxians (roh-BOT-zee-ansz)

Robotxians are Robloxians with Robotic or cyborg like attributes. Such as a mechanical arm, or looking like a robot themself. The only difference is that they still have the mind of a Robloxian, just the body is slightly different.

Usually, most of the time, a Robloxian transitions to a Robotxian. For example, an arm is gone, or a leg is gone. The Robloxian with a missing limb would go to a mechanic to give them a robot limb as replacement. They would now be classified as a Robotxian. But the different between a ROBOT and a Robotxian is that the Robloxian still contains the mind of a Robloxian, with only the appearance as a robot.

A simple robot is just a robot. A robotic Robloxian who doesn’t have a Robloxian mind, nor look like one.

The only way a Robotxian can have a full robot body and still be classified as a robotxian but not a robot itself, is if the robloxian’s mind is transported to the body.

The first Robotxian was created during ██/██/████, going by the name of [██████]. It was after a man was caught in a car accident, getting his arm severely injured. Society had the idea of replacing his arm with a robotic substitute. Eventually, over time they adapted. Some get artificial limbs to look cool, or they need to replace it.
